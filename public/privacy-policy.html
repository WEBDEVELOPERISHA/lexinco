Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Lexinco. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use our Legal Notice Generator service. By using our service, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use our service:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, contact number, and address provided when filling out the Legal Notice Generator form.
  • Dispute Details: Information about the dispute, including contract details, issue descriptions, and demands, as entered in the form.
  • Signature Data: Digital signatures provided via the signature pad.
  • Usage Data: Technical information such as IP address, browser type, and usage patterns, collected automatically when you interact with our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Generate and format legal notices based on the details you provide.
  • Deliver notices via email or WhatsApp, as requested.
  • Process payments for premium features (if applicable).
  • Improve our services and user experience through analytics.
  • Communicate with you regarding your account or support inquiries.

4. Data Storage and Security

We store your data securely on our servers, protected by industry-standard encryption and access controls. Personal information and dispute details are retained only for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. We take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or misuse of your data.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your data with:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties who assist in delivering our services, such as email delivery (e.g., SMTP services) or payment processing (e.g., Razorpay).
  • Legal Requirements: Authorities, if required by law or to protect our rights.

All third-party providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the purposes we specify.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing (where applicable).

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@lexinco.com.

7. Non-Legal Disclaimer

Lexinco is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice or legal services. The Legal Notice Generator is a tool designed to assist users in creating draft notices based on the information provided. These notices are for informational purposes only and should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before use. Lexinco is not responsible for the accuracy, legality, or consequences of the notices generated.

8. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies to enhance your experience, such as maintaining your session or analyzing usage patterns. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
